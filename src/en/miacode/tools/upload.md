# Chart upload

Open `Tools → Chart upload` to queue local charts and publish them to the server.

## Login

Enter your username and password. Systems with secure credential storage can remember them for later use.

## Prepare the queue

Choose a chart root folder and add it to the queue. Review chart folders, files, paths, and states. Move entries up or down, remove selected entries, or clear the queue.

Before uploading, review metadata and media in [Chart information](/en/miacode/chart/information), and inspect the [Analysis lists](/en/miacode/chart/validation).

## Results

Read progress and logs during upload. Completion shows successful, failed, and unconfirmed counts.

For a server-result-unconfirmed entry, check the server's actual publishing state before deciding what to do with it.

See [Chart download](/en/miacode/tools/download) to retrieve server charts.
