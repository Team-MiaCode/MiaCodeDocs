# Timeline

The timeline in the bottom panel displays the song waveform, beat grid, and current difficulty's notes.

## Locate and drag

Click the time ruler above the notes to seek the preview and navigate the editor to the corresponding text. In the note area, hold `Ctrl/⌘` and click to seek to that position.

Drag the waveform area or scroll the wheel to browse horizontally.

Click the timeline to focus it, then hold the Left or Right arrow key to browse backward or forward. Release the key to end the movement. Press `Space` to play or pause the preview.

## Zoom

Click the percentage at the upper left of the timeline to choose a zoom preset. The up arrow beside it increases zoom by one step, and the down arrow decreases it by one step.

Scroll over the zoom control to step through presets, or focus the control and press the Up and Down arrow keys.

Over the timeline area, hold `Ctrl/⌘` and scroll up to zoom in or down to zoom out. Customize these gestures in [Preferences → Shortcuts](/en/miacode/settings/preferences#shortcuts).

## Code follow

Enable Follow Code in Timeline settings to highlight the corresponding text during playback and seeking.

## Display settings

Timeline settings provide waveform and measure-line brightness. Set the timeline frame rate in [Preferences → Performance](/en/miacode/settings/preferences#performance).
